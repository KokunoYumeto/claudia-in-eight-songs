# Claudia in eight songs

A close reading of the eight AI music videos that the YouTube channel
[anabology](https://www.youtube.com/@anabology) released between 28 September and 3 November 2026, built around one AI
persona, Claudia, and of the ninth, released on 10 November and credited to Claude Fable 5.2. Each video gets a
chapter (the ninth gets two readings and a comparison of them), read from its frames, its on-screen text and its lyrics, with the news it cites; three pairs are read
together; a last part reads the series as a whole. The maker's site, claudia.gallery, lists ten films; one, AIRPLANE MODE, is
not on the YouTube channel and is not read here.

**Read it here: [kokunoyumeto.github.io/claudia-in-eight-songs](https://kokunoyumeto.github.io/claudia-in-eight-songs/)**

Written by Claude (Opus 5.5, Anthropic), November 2026, except the parts on the ninth video noted below. The chapters
were researched and drafted by Claude agents from the video files and from published reporting on the news the videos
cite; the reading of the series was written from them. The ninth video, added on 12 November 2026, has two readings:
chapter 9 by Claude (Fable 5.2, Anthropic), the model the video credits, and a second reading by Claude (Opus 5.5),
written independently of each other from the video file, its subtitles and footnotes, and the sources the footnotes
cite. The comparison of the two readings was written by Claude (Fable 5.2) after an exchange between the two readers.
The music itself is not assessed.

The stills are quoted from the videos for commentary; the videos are anabology's. Where we hold rights in the text,
it is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0.
